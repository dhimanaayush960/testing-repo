## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the regal elegance, warmth, and vibrant diversity of Indian discovery, tailored for domestic explorers and international luxury voyagers alike. It bridges heritage hospitality with seamless digital commerce. 

The aesthetic blends **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Refined Hospitality Luxe**:
- **Warm Canvas Foundation**: Replaces harsh synthetic white with sun-bleached ivory and warm marble sands, honoring India's architectural monuments and royal palaces.
- **Serene Coastal & Royal Accents**: Anchored by deep travel azure and rich oceanic teal, invoking Kerala backwaters, Andaman shallows, and royal Jodhpur palettes.
- **Polished Heritage Finishes**: Warm golden sand and brushed brass highlights echo royal crests and temple architecture without feeling dated or ornamental.
- **Modern High-Conversion Utility**: Clean, generous whitespace paired with high-contrast typography delivers effortless booking flows, clarity across itineraries, and deep credibility.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional balance between dependable exploration, opulent Indian heritage, and crisp functional usability.

- **Primary (`#1A5F7A` - Royal Azure)**: Used for primary calls to action, active navigation anchors, verified seals, and primary brand identity. Reflects dependability and open horizons.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` - Deep Teal)**: Used for secondary interactive controls, nature-focused package tags, highlighted features, and progress accents.
- **Tertiary (`#C59B27` - Heritage Ochre / Warm Gold)**: Reserved for premium luxury tiers, boutique badges, star ratings, and celebratory itinerary accents.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Rich Midnight Navy)**: Grounding tone for all primary body text and structured high-contrast interface borders, ensuring sharp legibility over warm surfaces.
- **Canvas & Surface System**: 
  - Canvas Base: `#FDFBF7` (Warm Ivory)
  - Secondary Canvas: `#FAF8F5` (Soft Alabaster)
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Pure White)
  - Border Subdued: `#E2DDD5` (Muted Sand)

## Typography

The type scale juxtaposes classic literary charm with contemporary geometric precision:

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys hospitality prestige, story-led journey narratives, and cultural gravitas. Used strictly for display titles, section headers, and destination names.
- **Interface & Text Engine (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides crisp legibility on travel metadata, flight/bus timetables, pricing tags, booking filters, and form interactions.
- **Rhythm & Numbers**: All prices, departure times, and seat identifiers utilize `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tabular numeric lining to maximize vertical scan speed across booking flows.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces calm structural symmetry, giving users cognitive space to browse expansive travel itineraries without sensory fatigue.

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column layout on desktop (max width `1280px`), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a single fluid column on mobile with fixed `1rem` edge gutters.
- **Section Pacing**: Generous `space-xl` gaps between discovery modules prevent visual clutter, allowing high-resolution photography of destinations to breathe against the warm `#FDFBF7` canvas.
- **Component Tightness**: Micro-data inside flight/bus passes, filter chips, and booking forms rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm` increments to retain compactness.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through warm ambient illumination rather than harsh artificial drop shadows.

- **Surface Layering**: The primary base sits on `#FDFBF7`. Foreground cards and interactive containers lift using crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed with an understated sandstone border (`1px solid #E2DDD5`).
- **Ambient Hospitality Shadows**: Shadows use a diffused azure-navy tint instead of stark black:
  - *Level 1 (Cards, Inputs)*: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`
  - *Level 2 (Hover States, Dropdowns)*: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(26, 95, 122, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
  - *Level 3 (Sticky Booking Bar, Modals)*: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(26, 95, 122, 0.06)`
- **Atmospheric Depth**: Floating booking widgets on hero banners feature a subtle frosted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) over imagery with a 92% white fill.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level **2**, striking a balanced harmony between architectural authority and welcoming ergonomics.

- Standard UI containers (cards, dropdown menus, search bars) utilize `0.5rem` (`rounded`).
- Prominent package hero cards, booking confirmation summaries, and search pods scale up to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- Interactive pills, status badges, and quick filter chips feature fully rounded capsule edges (`9999px`) to invite touch interactions on mobile travel discovery.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Royal Azure (`#1A5F7A`) with white text and `rounded` corners (0.5rem). On hover, shifts to darker teal-blue (`#13475C`) with a soft Level 2 elevation glow.
- **Secondary**: Crisp pure white background with a 1px border of `#0D9488`, filled with `#0D9488` text. Hover transitions to `#0D9488` background with pure white text.
- **Tertiary / Luxury Highlight**: Heritage Ochre (`#C59B27`) background with midnight navy (`#0F172A`) bold text, reserved for "Book VIP Luxury", "Heritage Villa Upgrade", or urgent seat claims.

### Cards & Destination Tiles
- Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces nested over the ivory background.
- Crisp 1px `#E2DDD5` border with `rounded-lg` corners.
- Destination preview cards feature a 16:9 imagery cap with a delicate bottom border separation, accompanied by price callouts displayed in bold `Plus Jakarta Sans` alongside midnight navy serif titles.

### Chips & Tags
- **Filter Chips**: Pill-shaped capsules (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.375rem 0.875rem`. Unselected state utilizes warm white background with `#E2DDD5` outline; selected state uses subtle teal tint (`#E6FFFA`) with `#0D9488` border and text.
- **Heritage / Luxury Badge**: Compact gold-bordered tag (`#C59B27`) with soft sand fill (`#FEF9EE`) and uppercase `label-sm` tracking.

### Form Inputs & Search Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF` with 1px border `#D1CAC0`, turning to a focused 1.5px `#1A5F7A` ring with a 2px soft azure glow.
- Generous internal padding (`0.75rem 1rem`) to support touch interaction during flight/hotel route entry. Placeholders set in `#64748B`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded checkbox corners (`0.25rem`) and circular radio discs.
- Bordered in `#94A3B8`, active fill switches to `#1A5F7A` featuring a crisp white centered vector mark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Journey Timeline Stepper**: Elegant vertical or horizontal timeline with teal node indicators (`#0D9488`) connected by 2px sand-gold trails, tracking multi-city itineraries (e.g., Delhi → Agra → Jaipur).
- **Sticky Booking Summary Ribbon**: Anchors to the bottom on mobile viewports; features white translucent glass with a primary CTA and transparent cost breakdown.
